Method. Every claim carries a source tag `[Sn]`; the Source Index at the bottom gives URL + date. "(snippet)" = taken from a search-result summary, page not opened. "(old)" = source predates 2025.

## 0. Headlines (read this first)

- Quiz/trivia/educational is a dead genre on Fortnite by player count: the most-played island tagged `quiz` has 21 players, `trivia` 15, `educational` 22, on a site where Pillars has 14,300 and Go Up For Brainrots 20,000 [S1][S2]. Pure quiz does not draw; quiz-inside-an-obby hybrids do slightly better (78 to 186 players) [S2][S3].
- The islands that win with 10-14 year olds are loop-and-collect (brainrot tycoons), last-one-standing party rounds (Pillars), and meme-able obbies (Only Up, +1 Speed, Hard Parkour) [S1][S2][S3].
- Epic Developer Rule 1.15: Epic "doesn't accept content that targets children under 13 as its primary audience" [S7]. Grades 6-8 are ages 11-14. Marketing wording matters; Rule 1.17 lets Epic grant discretionary exceptions for "educational" islands [S7].
- "Fortnite" in the title: no rule explicitly bans the word, but the Thumbnail Policy bans Epic "logos or product names" in thumbnails and Rules 3.1.1/3.1.3/3.1.5 forbid implying Epic endorsement and require a disclaimer on promo material [S7][S8]. See 3.9.
- Discover is being redesigned starting 2026-10-15 and new thumbnail/logo assets become mandatory 2026-10-22 [S10]. Deadline-relevant for any publish/update.
- Rocket Racing is dead (0 players on 2026-10-13) [S2][S36]. Fall Guys-type islands are small (top is 169 players) [S2].

## 3. Why islands spread among 10-14 year olds; Epic's rules for younger players

### 3.1 How Discover actually distributes (current docs) [S9]
- "Engagement drives distribution." Metrics: average playtime (capped at 120 min/session), CCU, **bounce rate** (sessions shorter than a row-specific threshold, often 5 min), player retention, **social play** (invites, returning in a party, % of time played in a party; metrics "coming to Project Analytics soon"), **QPTR** (play-through weighted by playtime), unique users (7 days) [S9].
- New islands are tested for up to 2 weeks; genre rows and personalised surfaces from day one [S9].
- You must pick **one genre**: Adventure & RPG, Battle Royale, **Deathrun & Platformer**, Horror, Music & Rhythm, **Party & Mini Games**, Roguelike, Roleplaying & Social, Shooter, Simulation & Tycoon, **Sport & Racing**, Strategy, Survival. No education genre exists. Misclassified islands are removed from genre rows [S9].
- Row order depends on the player's cohort: "age rating, platform, region, personalization opt-in" [S9]. Under-18 accounts default to personalised recommendations OFF, chat "Nobody", parties "Invite Only" (2022, old) [S41] (so For You under-reaches kids unless parents opt in; inference).
- "Highly similar" islands (title or thumbnail) are shown less; original developer is whoever first published the thumbnail, or the title on an island with 100 unique users in 96 h; originals "can only be used without restriction on a maximum of one live island at a time" [S9]. **Do not publish Grade 6/7/8 as separate islands with the same title/thumbnail.** (fnm's single slot with all cartridges already avoids this.)
- Highlights badges "New" and "Updated" on tiles [S9]. Search is hybrid keyword + semantic, ranked by CCU and confirmed recent search count [S9].

### 3.2 Discover Redesign and Spam Enforcement (Epic forum post, 2026-10-05) [S10]
- **2026-10-15**: phased rollout of redesigned Discover: edge-to-edge video-fed **hero carousel**; Epic's Picks tiles play a muted trailer on hover; art and transparent logo layered separately on tiles; **portrait tile + ranked Top 10 row**; optional muted **gameplay video** on the details page ("most rows send players to that page before the Lobby"); **like percentage** shown on tiles; "New"/"Updated" callouts. Content Pre-Checks start reviewing thumbnails; new submissions get a warning, published islands get a notification; "non-compliant thumbnails will hurt an island's Discover ranking" [S10].
- **2026-10-22**: portrait thumbnails, landscape thumbnails and logos **required to publish or update** an island. New assets: gameplay video, two portrait thumbnails (flat art+logo; art-only), art-only landscape thumbnail, transparent name logo. Existing island video is now "Trailer"; must be **General Audience rated** to be placed in the hero carousel or Epic's Picks [S10].
- Promo materials may only use outfits/items Epic owns or you have permission to use; no other companies' characters [S10].
- **Spam (Rule 1.9.1)**: "Do not publish multiple copies of the same island or metadata in an attempt to manipulate search ranking, engagement, or visibility from the same account, alternate accounts or teammate accounts." Announced 2026-03-05, enforced from 2026-03-11; penalties escalate (monetization, removal, suspension) [S11]. Since March: **81,000+ spam islands removed**; latest wave 44,535 duplicate islands and 707 accounts; new signals for duplicate content and alt accounts; penalties include permanent ban of the account and alts [S10].
- Earlier (2026-08, per tweet, snippet): Epic previewed like % next to CCU and a "Discover more upfront" redesign (Unreal Fest Chicago) [S10b]. The post says the Chicago talk's goal: players find "something worth playing" each time they open Discover [S10].

### 3.3 Likes, favorites, recommends
- v42.10 (2026-09-03): **Like, Dislike, Save, Follow** buttons on the in-game Overview tab, usable mid-game [S21]. v42.20 (2026-09-17): like/dislike from the details page after playing [S22]. Rule 1.9: do not "trick" players into favoriting or recommending [S7]. Asking honestly via end screen is not prohibited by the text but is a judgment call (inference).

### 3.4 Island codes, creator pages, marketing
- Epic's playbook: put the island code in everything you publish; use short gameplay trailer, key art, social rollout (TikTok, Instagram, X, Discord, YouTube) [S69]. Each creator has Fortnite.com/@CreatorCode with playlists; Support-a-Creator device was removed in 2023 so "players show support by playing" [S31].
- **Matchmaking Portals** gave OnlyUp a touchpoint outside Discover [S4].
- Streamer/TikTok pull: OnlyUp 110K CCU in 2023 was driven by organic influencer content ("reach the top as quickly as possible") plus Discover [S4]. Clip-ability matters. Secondary sources claim TikTok/Shorts are the main discovery path for kids (opinion) [S71].
- Creators can A/B test thumbnails and run a Sponsored row (paid) [S9]. Creator Portal (42.30) shows benchmarks vs top islands in genre for CTR, 5-minute bounce, session length, D1/D7 retention [S23].
- **Party-based gameplay**: Social Synergy API (`GetLocalParty()` party size, joins/leaves) shipped v42.10; docs headline "build gameplay that rewards playing together" [S21][S70].
- Epic's Picks: 4 islands a week; 3 pillars Innovation / Gameplay / Environment; replayability explicitly = "hidden secrets, being the best, multiple ways to interact"; applicants need a published island, GA-rated trailer, legal review of "core features and mechanics" [S12][S10]. A snippet claims "Arcade Games" were an Epic spotlight genre Jan-Jun 2026 (could not confirm in the fetched doc).

### 3.5 XP (Epic's "XP calibration" policy) [S14][S15]
- XP comes from **Accolades devices**. XP Award is a weight (Very Small to Very Large), not fixed XP; frequent accolades pay less, rare more [S14].
- Every published island is **calibrated**: measures how often players earn each accolade vs playtime; "needs a number of play sessions over time"; editing XP awards requires **republishing** to recalibrate [S14]. New islands show XP status "Analyzing" on fortnite.gg until calibrated, then "Awarding" [S3].
- Players should be able to earn most accolades "within a few hours"; award at most once a second is spam; **1 award per 2-10 minutes** suits most games; long games (>45 min) can award less often. Diminishing returns past a total-XP threshold across Creative, and for repeating the same game; developers cannot change that [S14].
- v40.30 (2026-04-30): multistage calibration shifts playtime XP gradually into accolade XP; small islands earn some accolade XP before full calibration; islands falling out of range step back one stage [S15].
- Rules: no "XP", "AFK", "coin farm" in name/description/thumbnail/lobby (1.13.3); don't imitate Epic's XP indicator visuals (1.13.1); no "XP/Experience/Battle Pass" in paid offers (4.4.4, 4.4.5); thumbnails may not reference levelling up or XP [S7][S8]. Epic's old news says players earn XP by playing any island (old) [S42b].
- Quest API (Verse, **experimental**, v41.10): quests appear in Fortnite's native quest UI; not yet publishable [S17][S19].

### 3.6 Age ratings and younger players
- IARC required to publish. Maximum ratings per region: ESRB **Teen**, PEGI 16, USK 16, ClassInd 16, GRAC 12+, Russia 12+, ACB Mature, IARC rest-of-world 16+ [S13]. Over-cap = not available in that region; over cap everywhere = blocked [S13]. (A search summary quoted PEGI 12 / IARC 12+ and the 2023 "E to Teen" framing; the fetched doc table is the one used here.)
- Parents set a rating limit in Parental Controls; islands above it show a lock and need the parental PIN [S13]. Parents can also turn Creative design off [S13].
- **Rule 1.15**: "We also don't accept content that targets children under 13 as its primary audience (no nursery rhymes). Images and text used to promote your island must be appropriate for a general audience (e.g. content that would merit a rating of ESRB Everyone 10+, or lower)." [S7]
- **Rule 1.17**: "We may permit exceptions at our discretion to our Fortnite Developer Rules if the primary purpose of your island is to provide an educational, scientific, artistic, or historical experience." [S7] (No published process; ask via Epic Player Support / Creator Portal ticket. Unverified.)
- **Rule 1.2**: no collecting or requesting personal info; no URL or QR code; no real-person photos; "don't provide other chat methods on your island, like chat boxes or custom keyboards" [S7]. So no typed-answer entry and no teacher dashboards linked from the island.
- **Rule 1.12**: "Do not include external links anywhere on your island." **1.2.1**: "No content or activities outside the island can impact inside the game experience." [S7]
- **Rule 1.11**: "Don't implement playable gambling content, raffles, or casino-style games ... whether or not they involve real money or virtual currency." [S7] Paid random items are separately allowed with disclosed odds, and restricted in the UK (under 18), Brazil, etc. [S28][S29]. Matters if you add wheels/crates.
- In-island transactions require 18+ publisher, Developer Program enrolment, and "Digital Purchases" disclosure; direct purchase prompts to minors are restricted (Canada under 13, UK under 16, France under 17, EU states) [S29][S30]. Irrelevant for a free island.
- Engagement payouts: eligibility needed 18+ and 90-day-old account (2023, old) [S31].
- **Cabined Accounts** (2022, old): players who report under-13 (or under the local consent age) have a limited account until a parent sets Parental Controls [S41]. School pilots with 11-12 year olds may hit parental-consent friction; verify current behaviour with a classroom test.
- **LLM Conversations** (publishable since 2026-07-30; v41.30): needs a parent opt-in "AI features" setting; cabined accounts need parent setup first; English only; voice audio not stored; rules 1.22: no medical/mental-health persona, no romantic persona, persona must suit island rating [S19][S24][S26][S7]. A kid-facing AI hype-man NPC would be gated per child by parents.

### 3.9 Using "Fortnite" in the title (island is currently called "Fortnite Math")
Quoted rules, [S7] (Epic Developer Rules, "Last updated: April 29, 2026"), https://legal.epicgames.com/fortnite/developer-rules :
- **3.1.1** "When promoting your island, you must not represent it as developed in partnership with Epic Games or suggest that it is in any way an official Epic Games Fortnite island, existing or past."
- **3.1.2** "When promoting your island, you may use the 'Developed in Fortnite' logo as long as it follows our Promoting Your Fortnite Experience Guidelines."
- **3.1.3** "You must not use the Fortnite or Epic Games logos anywhere in your island or in the promotional materials for your island."
- **3.1.5** "You must prominently include the following legal line on all promotional material: This is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Epic Games, Inc."
- **3.2.6** "...you must not purchase advertisements against keywords or search terms that are Epic Games trademarks, such as 'Fortnite' or 'Epic Games.'"
- **1.7** "The content you create, publish, sell, or use cannot infringe or violate someone else's rights, including their intellectual property rights (such as copyright or trademark)."
- **1.6** "Don't impersonate ... companies, brands, ... Epic employees, or anyone else."
- **1.13** "The assets (such as thumbnail images, descriptions, titles) you use to promote your Fortnite island must accurately represent the content within your island."
- **Thumbnail Image Policies** [S8] https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/thumbnail-image-policies : "Respect IP Ownership: Do not violate anyone's intellectual property rights, including Epic's. You can use Epic-owned assets made available to you in UEFN, but don't use recreated assets or **Epic Games' logos or product names**."

Reading: no sentence says "the island title may not contain the word Fortnite". Evidence it is tolerated: live islands use it, e.g. "Only Up Fortnite!" (113,933 peak, Everyone rated) and "Fortnite Trivia" [S3]. Risks: "Fortnite" is a product name, which the thumbnail policy bans in thumbnails; 3.1.1 bans suggesting an official island; "Fortnite Math" reads like a brand extension; 3.1.5 requires the not-endorsed legal line on promo material (title/description are promo metadata). The licensed-brand pages (LEGO: no LEGO wordmark in title; Squid Game allows the name but no endorsement implication) show Epic's pattern of policing titles (snippet) [S35c]. Recommendation (judgment, not a rule): drop "Fortnite" from the title and thumbnail text; add the 3.1.5 line to the description. I did not find a Creator Portal metadata check that blocks the word.

### What drives repeat play and talk-to-friends (supported by the table, interpretation marked)
- **Economy between questions** (Gimkit shop, Blooket tokens, Wayground power-ups): the question is the entry fee; the meta-game is the point.
- **Steal/sabotage** (Gold Quest, Crypto Hack, Strike and Shield, Glitch): produces stories and trash talk (interpretation; reviewers cite it, no study).
- **Collectibles with visible rarity** (Blooks 8 tiers, Prodigy pets).
- **Comeback mechanics** (Streak Saver, Immunity, Redemption, Mastery Peak, Time Penalty set by host).
- **Co-op escape and team scoring** (One Way Out, Blastball, Team mode).
- **Teacher-visible live board** and **teacher chaos button** (Wayground Glitch, rate-limited).
- **Student-authored content** (KitCollab).
- **Asynchronous homework mode with the same sets** (Blooket, Gimkit, Kahoot Assignments): lets the game continue outside class.

## 7. Takeaways for a math race (25 bullets)

1. **Put the persistent number on the HUD** (station N/10 as an altitude meter plus run timer, best time beside it). Every long-lived hit has one. [Only Up, +1 Speed, Go Up For Brainrots] [S3][S4]
2. **Cut the first-5-minutes risk.** Bounce under ~5 min is a Discover metric; first door, first comic penalty and first reward must land inside the first minute; default the grade/skill menu to a pre-highlighted choice (menu-first starts are a drop-off risk, inference). [Discover docs] [S9]
3. **Offer a 3-4 minute "sprint" playlist next to the full course.** Party genre = "a round ends, someone wins, everyone plays again". [Pillars, Fall Guys] [S9][S3][S76]
4. **Add a free, timer-less random power-up on correct answers** (shield, speed, "freeze the leader"). Random item cadence is the core of Pillars; skip purchase/skip-wait wheels, which drew the "gambling" backlash and run into Rule 1.11. [Pillars, Steal The Brainrot] [S3][S5][S7]
5. **Strike and Shield for doors:** let a streak earn the right to fire a wrong-door penalty (yeet, freeze, dizzy) at the leader; shield absorbs one. [Wayground Classic, Blooket Gold Quest] [S52][S46]
6. **Second chance after a penalty:** a "redemption" re-ask of a missed door later in the run with bonus time back. [Wayground Redemption, Blooket Mastery ordering] [S53][S45]
7. **Host-style penalty tuning:** per-class setting for how long a wrong-door penalty lasts (Time Penalty for Incorrects). [Blooket] [S45]
8. **Streak Saver / Immunity as collectibles on the course**, spent instead of a penalty. [Wayground, Gimkit shop] [S51][S47]
9. **Co-op mode** where team time = sum or slowest member: ties into party-play metrics. [One Way Out, Blastball, Wayground Team, Social Synergy API] [S48][S52][S21]
10. **Medal tiers from a par time** (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Author) shown before the run; add an accuracy medal separately from speed. [Trackmania, Kahoot Accuracy mode] [S61][S50]
11. **Ghost of your personal best** (semi-transparent, no collision) and a "staff ghost" for the par time. Feasibility in UEFN not verified (record position samples in persistable data, size limits not stated in the doc read). [Mario Kart, Trackmania] [S63][S62][S42]
12. **Persist best time and medals per player**, validate with required checkpoints, show in the lobby dialog. Epic's own template does this. [Speedway template] [S43]
13. **Daily course seed** (same questions for everyone, one featured run per day) plus a weekly/term reset of the class board; the native board only lists players present in-session. [Trackmania TOTD, Wordle, Rocket League] [S62][S65][S43]
14. **Make the finish screen screenshot-ready:** medal, time, per-station tick row, no URL or QR (Rule 1.2). Shareable, spoiler-free artefact = peer discovery. [Wordle] [S65][S7]
15. **Design the 15-second clip:** the titan/laser finale and the comic penalties are the TikTok moments; Only Up spread because creators raced to reach the top fast. Put the island code on the finale screen and trailer. [Only Up, Epic playbook] [S4][S69]
16. **Collectibles with visible rarity**, persisted across sessions (trails, penalty-effect variants, finish poses), earned by play, never bought. Avoid mentioning rewards/V-Bucks in metadata (Rule 1.13.2). [Blooket Blooks, Prodigy pets] [S46][S55][S7]
17. **A random-perk choice after each station** (roguelike: "choose from random rewards after each area"). Escape [Roguelike] has 167M minutes with this loop. [Escape Roguelike] [S3]
18. **Teacher chaos button** from the projector view: a rate-limited "glitch/fog the whole class" (once per ~90 s). [Wayground Glitch] [S51]
19. **Question authorship by students happens outside the island** (no typed input in Fortnite, Rule 1.2): feed student-written questions into the cartridge pipeline. [Gimkit KitCollab] [S47][S7]
20. **Genre choice is a strategic decision:** Deathrun & Platformer (crowded, clone-heavy), Party & Mini Games (round loop; Pillars-dominated), Sport & Racing (mostly driving RP). No education genre exists; do not tag "educational" to gain rows. [Discover genres] [S9][S2]
21. **Rename and re-skin the metadata now:** drop "Fortnite" from the title and thumbnail text, add the Rule 3.1.5 not-endorsed line to the description, never write "XP", prepare the new portrait/landscape/logo/video assets before 2026-10-22, keep a GA-rated trailer. [Developer Rules, Discover redesign] [S7][S8][S10]
22. **Frame the audience as 10+/general** and be ready to invoke Rule 1.17 for an educational exception; keep promo art E10+; remember kids under 13 are cabined and may need parent setup. [Rule 1.15/1.17, Cabined accounts] [S7][S41]
23. **Quiet XP:** if you use Accolades, award once per 2-10 minutes (per station or medal), republish once content is stable so calibration can run, expect "Analyzing" for a while. [Accolades, v40.30] [S14][S15][S3]
24. **Keep one island, update it visibly:** Epic's duplicate detection penalises near-identical titles/thumbnails across islands; "Updated" badges and meaningful updates help; abandoned v1 maps fade. [Discover docs, Hard Parkour v1] [S9][S3]
25. **If using Fall Guys template, plan for E rating:** Bean avatars, no armed hostile guards; if guards stay, skip the template. Market there is small anyway. [Fall Guys] [S34][S35][S2] (inference on guards)
